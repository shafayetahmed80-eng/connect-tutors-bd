|---|---|---|

| Guardian Settings reference (`4Hcqc2ZqkgMG`) | Separate setting areas appear for name, phone number, password, profile verification, lock/unlock, and account deletion. | Current planned Settings placeholder must not become a cosmetic tab. | Job-detail reference (`yzVtF-xTdUIU`) | Detail view includes a dynamic title, Job ID, posted date, tuition type, Tutor and student gender, days/time, student count, salary, subjects, location, a free-text requirement, Direction, and Apply. | The reference includes a specific area label and free text. Current area-level maps and no-free-text public projection are safer; an apply CTA is appropriately replaced by authenticated Tutor interest with no Guardian contact disclosure. |

## Safety and product constraints carried into review

- Guardian phone, email, student identity, exact address, and free-text notes must remain outside the public/Tutor Job Board projection.
- Admin publication continues to require a recorded Guardian confirmation call; Admin 2FA remains mandatory.
- The reference images are design inspiration only, not authorization to copy unverifiable badges, rankings, rewards, live counts, or third-party branding.
