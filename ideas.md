# Connect Tutors BD — Reference-led Design Specification

## Ground-Truth Reference

This project is a faithful, original-code recreation of the public visual structure and user journey of `meettutorbd.com`, rebranded as **Connect Tutors BD** / **Connecttutorsbd.com**. The reference dictates the information architecture: a slim contact strip, logo-led header with pill navigation, a light-blue rounded hero, tutoring categories, trust statistics, the five-step tutoring flow, FAQ, contact footer, and a multi-step tutor-request journey. The project will use fresh branded artwork, distinct wording, custom iconography, and its own Connect Tutors BD logo rather than copying source code, proprietary graphics, photographs, or customer testimonials.

## Chosen Design Direction — Neighbourhood Learning Blue

### Design Movement

The direction is a contemporary **Bangladeshi education-service landing page**, combining the clarity of modern civic design with the friendly editorial softness of family-oriented learning brands. It retains the reference site’s airy blue-and-white visual cadence while giving Connect Tutors BD a recognisable own identity.

### Core Principles

1. **Trust is visible:** clear contact pathways, restrained statistics, verified-status cues, and transparent request steps should make a guardian feel informed before taking action.
2. **Learning feels human:** family photography, rounded containers, personable illustrations, and generous whitespace should avoid a cold marketplace experience.
3. **Action is never hidden:** primary calls to action appear consistently in the header, hero, and supporting content, with a clear distinction between hiring a tutor and applying as one.
4. **Responsive calm:** mobile views preserve hierarchy through a single-column reading rhythm, large tap targets, and a compact expandable navigation.

### Color Philosophy

The site centers on **Connected Sky** (`#1677E8`), an ownable, confident blue that conveys reliability and educational progress without looking corporate. It is supported by mist-blue backgrounds for spaciousness, deep ink for confident readability, warm saffron as a small reward/achievement accent, and leaf green only for reassuring online/verified signals. Bright, low-key imagery supports dark ink typography; text is never placed directly over unprotected photography.

### Layout Paradigm

The experience follows a **guided learning path** instead of a generic central grid: the hero leads into a horizontal proof ribbon, category cards, a full-width confidence statement, then the vertically connected five-step process. Alternating warm white and mist-blue fields create clear chapter breaks and direct the eye to action.

### Signature Elements

1. A rising **two-link orbit mark** represents a student and tutor meeting in motion.
2. Soft sky-blue **capsule navigation** and outline chips echo the accessible, friendly service model.
3. A fine **dotted route line** joins the five matching steps, turning tutor selection into an understandable journey.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction is direct and encouraging. Buttons have a quick tactile press response, cards lift subtly to confirm focus, carousels use deliberate controls, and form steps reveal only what the user needs next. Motion supports comprehension rather than decoration.

### Animation

With reduced-motion preferences respected, the hero imagery rises into place with a short opacity-and-transform transition, proof items stagger in at 50 ms intervals, and cards use 180 ms transform/shadow transitions. Accordion panels open in under 250 ms. No looping or distractive animation is used.

### Typography System

**Manrope** provides the crisp, open body and interface text, while **DM Sans** in heavy weights anchors headings and numeric proof points. Headlines use compact negative tracking at large sizes; body copy stays comfortably open with a 1.6 line-height. Bengali copy uses the compatible `Noto Sans Bengali` fallback where included in future content.

### Brand Essence

**Connect Tutors BD makes finding a suitable tutor in Bangladesh feel guided, verified, and personal.** Its personality is **reassuring, capable, and warm**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are clear and directional; calls to action feel supportive rather than sales-heavy; microcopy reduces uncertainty with concrete timing and process cues. Example lines: “A better tutor match begins here.” and “Share what your child needs — we’ll do the matching.” Generic greetings and vague calls to action are excluded.

### Wordmark & Logo

The logo combines a custom two-part interlocking orbit icon with a confident, slightly rounded “Connect” wordmark and a smaller blue “Tutors BD” companion. The standalone mark is bold enough for favicon and header use without relying on text.
